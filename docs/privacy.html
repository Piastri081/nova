Privacy

Your data, in plain language.

Last updated: October 31, 2026

What Nova stores

When you sign in, Nova stores the email address or phone number you enter, a display name, your profiles (name, avatar color, kids flag, and an optional hashed PIN), your My List, and your playback progress. Session tokens are stored as hashes. Login codes and PINs are never stored in plain text.

Why we store it

This data is required to sign you in, keep separate profiles, sync your list and Continue Watching across devices, and restore your session. Nova does not sell data, show ads, or use your viewing history for advertising.

Where it is stored

The hosted Nova service runs on Cloudflare Workers and Cloudflare D1. Data is processed by Cloudflare on our behalf. Email codes are delivered through Resend; SMS codes are delivered through Twilio. Those providers receive the destination address/number and the message content needed to deliver your code.

Retention

Account and profile data is kept while your account exists. Login codes expire after ten minutes and are removed after use. Sessions expire after 30 days. Progress and watchlist entries are kept until you remove them or delete the profile.

Deleting your data

You can delete a profile in the app. To delete your whole account, open Profile → Delete Account. This permanently removes your account, all profiles, My List entries, playback progress, sessions, and pending login codes. If you cannot sign in, contact the project using the repository link in the footer and include the email address or phone number you used.

Children

Nova is intended for users aged 13 and older. Kids profiles are a content-filtering convenience, not a verified parental-consent system. Do not create an account for a child under 13.

Security

Traffic uses HTTPS. Session tokens are random 256-bit values stored in the iOS Keychain on your device and as SHA-256 hashes on the server. Profile PINs are salted and hashed, with rate limiting and temporary lockout after repeated failures. No system is perfectly secure; use a device passcode and keep iOS updated.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated date above will change. Material changes will be noted on the project's release page.
